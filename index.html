✅ What Is DSH Console?

DSH Console is a user-friendly desktop application that lets you build and run smart programs called "spatiotemporal agents." These agents can think, learn, and act based on location-based data (that's the "spatiotemporal" part!). You can use it for projects like tracking wildlife, mapping city traffic, analyzing weather patterns, or even creating your own AI assistant that understands geography.

The best part? Everything runs locally on your computer, so your data stays private and you don't need an internet connection to use it. It's like having a mini AI supercomputer right in your pocket—well, on your desktop!
